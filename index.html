Quality

What happens before a lot ships

A peptide is only useful to a lab if the lab knows exactly what it is. These are the checks each lot passes through, in order.

A certificate of analysis lists the compound, lot number, purity by HPLC, observed mass and test date. Ask for the certificate for any lot before you order.

  1. 1

    Synthesis

    The chain is built one amino acid at a time by solid-phase synthesis, then cleaved from the resin.

  2. 2

    Purification

    Preparative HPLC separates the full-length peptide from truncated and deleted sequences.

  3. 3

    Analysis

    Analytical HPLC reports purity. Mass spectrometry confirms the molecular weight matches the sequence.

  4. 4

    Lyophilization

    The purified peptide is freeze-dried to a stable powder and sealed in vials.

  5. 5

    Lot release

    Each vial is labeled with its lot number, and the certificate for that lot is filed and sent with the order.

FAQ

Common questions

What does "research use only" mean?

The products are chemical reagents for laboratory and in-vitro research. They have not been evaluated or approved for use in people or animals, and they are not sold for that purpose.

Do you give dosing or usage advice?

No. We can answer questions about the material itself, such as its identity, purity and storage. We cannot advise on any use in humans or animals.

How are the peptides supplied?

Most are lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder in sealed vials. A few items, such as bacteriostatic water, are sterile solutions. The amount stated on the label is the peptide content of that vial.

How should lyophilized peptides be stored?

Keep vials sealed, dry and protected from light. Lyophilized peptides are generally stored frozen at −20 °C for long-term stability. Follow the storage line on your lot's certificate if it differs.

What is a certificate of analysis?

A document issued for one specific lot. It lists the compound, lot number, purity measured by HPLC, the mass observed by mass spectrometry, and the date of testing.

Who can order?

Laboratories, institutions and qualified researchers. We ask you to confirm research use when you send an inquiry.
